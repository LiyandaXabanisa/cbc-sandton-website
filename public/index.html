Open Doors

Our vision

To minister and serve the whole person, to build a nation of people utilizing the principles of God’s word, which will lead to the lives of people being changed for the better, for excellence and for empowerment.

Change Bible Church Sandton carries this same vision as a branch of Change Bible Church, extending it across the city one open door at a time.

Register your details and someone from CBC Sandton will follow up. Whether you’re visiting this Sunday, joining a small group, or just want to be added to our list, we would love to hear from you.
