Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

Change Bible Church Sandton (“CBC Sandton”) is a branch of Change Bible Church. We are the responsible party for the personal information collected on this website. Physical address: 76 Bevan Road, Edenburg, Sandton, Johannesburg, Gauteng, 2128. Contact: admin@cbcsandton.co.za or 060 202 3377. Our Information Officer, the person responsible for looking after your information, can be reached on the same details.

What we collect

  • Your name, and your email address and/or phone number, when you register.
  • Anything extra you choose to tell us in the optional note.
  • The date and time you gave your consent.
  • If you activate a member login: your email address and a password. We cannot see your password, because it is stored in scrambled form by our database provider.

We do not collect or store payment card details. Tithes and offerings are paid on our payment provider’s own page, which has its own privacy terms.

Why we collect it

To welcome you, keep in touch about church life, and manage our membership and follow-up. We do this because you ticked the consent box when you registered. We do not sell your information or use it for anything unrelated.

Who can see it

Authorised leaders and administrators of CBC Sandton. Your details are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in a secure data centre outside South Africa. We keep it there under appropriate safeguards, and we also keep a separate encrypted backup that only church leaders can open.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are connected to the church, or until you ask us to remove it. If you leave the church, we remove your details when we no longer need them.

Children

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must register for you and give consent on your behalf.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent at any time, by contacting us on the details above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
